Once it was paper charts. Today it is the smartphone.

Boating has always been about freedom.

But the way we plan and navigate has changed. Real-time weather data, digital nautical charts, community tips for harbors, and now even buoy booking by app.

Digitalization does not take the magic out of a trip. It takes away the stress.

Here are five apps that really make sense on board.

## 1. Savvy Navvy - Navigation rethought

**Ideal for:** Simple route planning and clear nautical charts

Savvy Navvy is often described as "Google Maps for boating" - and that is a pretty good fit.

The app combines:

- digital nautical charts
- weather data
- tides
- automatic route calculation

You enter a starting point and destination, then get a route with distance and estimated time. This is especially helpful for coastal trips or unfamiliar cruising grounds.

What makes Savvy Navvy strong is its intuitive handling. No technical overload, just a clear overview.

Of course, responsibility still stays with the skipper. But as a planning tool, the app is very well thought out.

## 2. Windy - Understand the weather instead of guessing

**Ideal for:** Wind, gusts, thunderstorms, pressure development

Windy is extremely detailed and especially useful when the weather starts to change.

You can see:

- wind direction and strength
- gust peaks
- precipitation
- air pressure development

This is worth a lot when spending the night at anchor or on a buoy. If you spot wind shifts early, you sleep more calmly.

## 3. Navily - Harbors, bays, and reviews

**Ideal for:** First-hand reports and spot research

Navily works like a community map for skippers.

You can find:

- reviews of bays
- notes on seabed and shelter
- photos
- comments on infrastructure

The exchange between skippers is especially helpful. It gives you a better feel for what to expect.

Still, the same rule applies here: check information and do not rely on it blindly.

## 4. SmartBuoy - Buoy booking made easy

**Ideal for:** Plannable moorings and sustainable use

While many apps provide information, SmartBuoy solves a very specific problem: uncertainty when searching for a place to stay.

With the SmartBuoy app, you can:

- see available buoys
- book directly
- get clearly defined mooring places
- receive digital confirmation

This reduces search traffic in crowded bays and creates structure instead of chaos.

Especially in heavily used cruising areas, that means:

- less stress on arrival
- more safety in planning
- more sustainability through organized use

The app complements navigation and weather perfectly because it simplifies the last step of the day: mooring safely.

## 5. PredictWind or similar routing apps

**Ideal for:** Longer trips and strategic planning

For more ambitious sailors, routing apps such as PredictWind offer deeper models.

They combine:

- weather data
- currents
- polar diagrams
- optimal route calculation

They are not always essential for coastal trips, but they are very helpful on longer passages.

## Digital tools that keep your back clear

Some people say too much technology takes away the romance. In reality, it is the other way around.

Digital tools take care of organization and analysis.

You keep your focus on what matters.

Instead of searching nervously for a free buoy at dusk, you enjoy the sunset. Instead of guessing wind shifts, you recognize them early.

Digitalization does not mean dependence. It means preparation.

## Conclusion: The right mix matters

No app replaces experience.

But the right combination saves time, nerves, and sometimes money.

A good nautical chart. A reliable weather app. A community platform. And a solution for plannable moorings.

That turns a good trip into a relaxed trip.

And that is exactly what being on the water is about.
